Lesson 10 Creative Build

Animated Scene

Use HTML and CSS to create a scene with independent animated parts. Replace the example idea with your own if you want.

Scene Rules

  1. Animate at least four independent elements.
  2. Use at least one transform-based movement.
  3. Use at least one multi-step @keyframes sequence.
  4. Make at least one animation loop smoothly.
  5. Add a reduced-motion version.
